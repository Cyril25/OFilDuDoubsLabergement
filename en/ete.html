Summer by the lakes

Lake Saint-Point, Lake Remoray and the cool air of the Jura Mountains

1 km from Lake Remoray and 4 km from Lake Saint-Point, the third largest natural lake in France, the apartment is ideal for a cool summer in the Jura Mountains: swimming, paddleboarding, hiking, cycling, cheese dairy visits… and dinners on the large balcony overlooking the Doubs.

Swimming

1 km away, the small beach at the Labergement leisure area on Lake Remoray offers natural swimming (unsupervised). Lake Saint-Point, 5 minutes away, has many access points to the water around Malbuisson.

1 km – 4 km

Paddleboarding, sailing and boat trips

Paddleboards, pedal boats, sailing boats and kayaks can be rented in season at the Malbuisson and Les Grangettes water sports centres. For a relaxing outing, board the electric boats on Lake Saint-Point.

4 km – 7 km

Nature and viewpoints

The Lake Remoray nature reserve and its visitor centre, the Maison de la Réserve, are just a stone's throw away. Climb to the Belvédère des 2 Lacs for the sunset, to Mont d'Or for the view of the Alps, and go on to the source of the Doubs or the Saut du Doubs waterfall.

1 km – 55 km

Hiking and cycling

Dozens of walking and cycling routes start around the apartment. In Métabief, several rental shops offer e-mountain bikes to explore the trails of Mont d'Or, and a paragliding flight shows you the lakes from the sky.

See the routes

With the family

In summer, the Métabief resort becomes a leisure park: summer toboggan run, zip line, tree-top adventure course. Children will also love the Ferme de la Batailleuse farm, the Coni'Fer steam train and the Sentiers du Morbier game trail starting from the Fruitière des Lacs.

1 km – 8 km

Local produce

Every morning from 9 to 10:30 am, watch Comté and Morbier being made at the Fruitière des Lacs, 1 km away. Honey from the Rucher des Deux Lacs, Comté ripening at Fort Saint-Antoine and absinthe from Pontarlier round off the experience.
