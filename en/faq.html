Frequently asked questions

Everything you need to know before booking

Answers to the questions we are asked most often. Can't find yours? Write to us or give us a call, we will be happy to help.

How many guests can stay?

Up to 4 guests, in two bedrooms each with a double bed. A travel cot and a high chair are available for babies.

What are the check-in and check-out times?

Check-in between 5 PM and 10 PM, with keys handed over in person or via a key box depending on our availability. Check-out before 11 AM.

Are pets allowed?

Yes, on prior request, with a supplement of €40 per stay.

Are linen and cleaning included?

Bed linen and towels are provided. The cleaning fee is €40 per stay (flat rate).

Is parking easy?

Yes, free parking spaces are available right outside the building.

Is the apartment accessible?

The apartment is on the first floor with no lift: 16 steps, with a handrail.

Is smoking allowed? Can we have a party?

The apartment is non-smoking indoors (smoking is allowed on the balcony) and parties are strictly forbidden.

Where is the apartment?

In the heart of Labergement-Sainte-Marie, 1 km from Lake Remoray, 4 km from Lake Saint-Point and Malbuisson and 8 km from Métabief. Full distances and directions on our Location & access page.

How can I book?

Directly with us via the contact form, by phone or WhatsApp, or online on Airbnb and Booking.

Can we come without a car?

Yes. Shuttles link Frasne station (Paris – Lausanne TGV) to Labergement-Sainte-Marie, and once here the lake, the shops and several visits are within walking distance. Details on our Location & access page.

Are there shops and restaurants nearby?

Yes, in the village and within walking distance: grocery store, cheese dairy, bakery, restaurants and food trucks. See our Shops and Where to eat pages.

What is there to do nearby?

At Lake Remoray, 1 km away: swimming, fishing from a rowing boat (rental at the village newsagent), a hike around the lake and an easy walk or bike ride around the nearby pond. Also swimming and water sports on Lake Saint-Point, skiing in Métabief in winter, cheese dairies and visits all year round. Our ideas on the Activities, Hikes and Events pages.
